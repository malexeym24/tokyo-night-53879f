# Roppongi / Nishi-Azabu / Azabu-Juban / Azabudai - Friday night notes

Checked: 2026-10-09, about 22:25 JST. Venue data is in `roppongi.json` (7 venues).

## What the area is like on a Friday (for a 43-year-old solo, upscale profile)
- **Roppongi crossing / Gaien-higashi-dori.** This is the loud core. Touts (kyakuhiki) work the street, and there are hostess and "girls bar" fronts plus cheap student clubs. Do not follow anyone from the street.
- **Grand Hyatt / Roppongi Hills, Tokyo Midtown (Ritz-Carlton), Azabudai Hills (Janu).** These are calm and premium, with hotel bars aimed at 30s-60s. Socializing is easiest at a bar counter (Oak Door, Maduro counter) and harder at the Ritz.
- **Roppongi 7-chome back streets.** Roku Nana and R3 are about 5 minutes on foot from the Grand Hyatt, so bar hopping on foot works: Oak Door -> Maduro -> Roku Nana -> club. Janu and the Ritz are about 15 minutes' walk or a short taxi from the Hyatt (estimate).
- **Nishi-Azabu.** This is a quieter, more "insider" late-bar district about 10 minutes' walk west of the crossing. I could not confirm any of its bars for 2026 in the time available (see below).
- **Getting there.** The Hibiya line runs directly from Akihabara to Roppongi. A taxi back late at night is the realistic option. I did not verify the fare or the last-train time.

## Scams / safety (sources)
- The US State Dept OSAC Japan report (5 Dec 2025) cites drink spiking, theft of cash and cards, and inflated bar tabs enforced by threats in entertainment areas including Roppongi: https://www.osac.gov/Content/Report/0372abfc-517c-493d-87e7-1ee6b159615b
- The US Embassy warned about Roppongi bar druggings in 2009 (Stars and Stripes): https://www.stripes.com/news/2009-03-19/us-embassy-warns-americans-to-avoid-visiting-bars-and-clubs-in-roppongi-area-1989043.html
- The usual pattern is that a street tout leads you to a bar, then the drink is spiked or the bill is inflated. Rules for tonight:
  - Go only to venues you chose yourself.
  - Never go with touts or with women who approach you on the street.
  - Check the price before ordering.
  - Carry one card and limited cash.
