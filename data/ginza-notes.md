# Ginza / Marunouchi / Toranomon - notes (checked 2026-10-09, ~22:25 JST)

## Roppongi vs Ginza on a Friday night (43, solo, upscale, wants to meet people)

| | Roppongi | Ginza / Toranomon |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting people | Better. Long-standing hub for expats and international visitors, English easy, mixed locals/expats/visitors (Japan Royal Service, LIVE JAPAN) | Harder. Refined, small counter bars, limited seating, quiet; no source describes an expat mingling scene |
| Solo | Fine, many bars/lounges, plus Fri/Sat pub-crawl format exists | Excellent for solo cocktail at a counter, but you talk to the bartender, not the room |
| Atmosphere | Louder, clubs till dawn; touts on main strip, drink-spiking warnings in lower-end clubs (JREF, Japan Royal Service) | Polished, expensive: seat charge ¥1,000-3,000 and cocktails ¥1,800-3,500 reported (guide quoted in search) |
| Walkability | Dense, bars within a few blocks of Roppongi crossing | Ginza bars walkable; Toranomon/Yaesu hotel bars are separate taxi hops |
| From Akihabara | ~20-25 min taxi | Ginza ~10 min taxi; Tokyo Sta./Yurakucho 1-2 JR stops |

**Bottom line (sources + judgment):** for socialising, Roppongi wins. In this zone the realistic social options tonight are **Gold Bar at EDITION Toranomon** (weekend DJs per 2022 launch info, till 02:00) and **Zouk Tokyo** (club, 22:00-04:30, likely younger crowd). Punch Room (till 02:00) is the stylish late Ginza option. Ginza = one great cocktail, then move.

**Hostess clubs - excluded:** Ginza 6-8 chome has many "club" (クラブ) hostess venues; anything signed クラブ with a door attendant and no menu is likely one. None are in ginza.json.

**Tonight timing (22:25):** The ROOF closes 23:00 (skip). Peter and Bvlgari close ~24:00. Andaz ~01:00 (unverified). Punch Room / Gold Bar to 02:00. Zouk to 04:30.
